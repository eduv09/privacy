Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 May 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the Personal Finance Dashboard handles information when you use it. This is a personal, non-commercial application built and operated by a single developer for their own private use. It is not a commercial product or service offered to the public.

2. What Data Is Accessed

The application connects to Millennium BCP through the Enable Banking platform, which uses the Open Banking (PSD2) standard. The only data retrieved is:

  • Bank account balances
  • Bank account transaction history

This access is strictly read-only — the application can never initiate payments, transfers, or any write operations on your bank account.

3. How Data Is Stored

All fetched bank data is stored exclusively on your local machine (e.g., in your browser's local storage or a local database). It is never transmitted to, or stored on, any remote server, cloud service, or third party.

4. Third-Party Sharing

No personal data is shared with any third party. The only external service involved is Enable Banking, which acts as the regulated PSD2 intermediary between this application and Millennium BCP. Enable Banking's own privacy practices are governed by their separate privacy policy.

5. Tracking & Analytics

This application does not use:

  • Cookies of any kind
  • Analytics or telemetry services
  • Advertising networks
  • User accounts or registration
  • Any form of tracking or profiling

6. Purpose of Processing

The sole purpose of accessing and processing your bank data is personal financial management — viewing your own account information for your own benefit. No data is processed for commercial, marketing, or any other purpose.

7. Legal Basis (GDPR)

This application is governed by Portuguese law and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (Regulation (EU) 2016/679). The legal basis for processing is legitimate interest of the sole user (the developer) accessing their own financial data for personal use, as well as contractual necessity with Enable Banking for PSD2 access.

As the sole data subject and controller, you retain all rights granted by the GDPR, including the right to access, rectify, and erase your data — all of which you exercise directly by managing the local files on your machine.

8. Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as you keep it on your local machine. You may delete it at any time by clearing your browser's local storage or removing the relevant local files.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to the repository owner via GitHub.
